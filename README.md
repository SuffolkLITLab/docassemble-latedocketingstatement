# Enter a Massachusetts civil appeal late

Help a person without a lawyer ask to enter a civil appeal late in the Massachusetts Appeals Court. They must already have filed a notice of appeal and received a notice of assembly. The interview makes a motion, supporting affidavit, certificate of service, and filing instructions. Users may attach supporting documents and must print and sign their papers.

This is not a late notice of appeal or late Civil Docketing Statement. Criminal cases and appeals already dismissed by the trial court are outside its scope. A pending dismissal motion triggers guidance about the order of steps. The interview does not file papers; fee-waiver papers are separate.

Entry point: `docassemble.latedocketingstatement:data/questions/late_docketing_statement.yml`.

The [Appeals Court's single-justice guidance](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/single-justice-practice) controls the steps and fee. The interview also links MassLegalHelp's [fee-waiver instructions](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/courts-hearings-language-rights/courts-massachusetts/how-fill-out-affidavit-indigency) and [legal-help directory](https://www.masslegalhelp.org/find-lawyer).
